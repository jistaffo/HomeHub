🔴 Highest-impact finding: Service Line Coverage is age-tiered. Your $10,000 Service Line Coverage limit (HO-2442.2) only applies if the failed line is under 50 years old. This house was built in 1969 — any original water, sewer, gas, or electrical service line is 57 years old. Unless you can document a line was replaced, a failure on an original line is capped at just $2,500, not $10,000. See Action Items below — this directly connects to the sewer scope and electrical panel work already on your list.
🟠 Personal Property is "Limited Replacement Cost" (B1), not full replacement cost. If an item is damaged or stolen, you must actually repair or replace it within 3 years to get full replacement value — otherwise State Farm only pays actual cash value (depreciated). File claims promptly and keep receipts for anything you replace.
